# Review Copilot Launch Narrative

## The problem

AI-generated documents are being reviewed as raw diffs even when the decision depends on reading flow, accuracy, and stakeholder language.

## The product answer

Commentary opens the rendered document first, keeps source tools nearby, and anchors comments to meaningful review context.

## The launch ask

Approve a controlled rollout for documentation-heavy teams with support coverage and rollback ownership in place.

## Decision

Launch after the support FAQ, customer memo, and migration report pass review.
